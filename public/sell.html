FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a Google API key to use it? ▼
No! LeadFlow has a built-in Instant Lead Generator that works without any API key. If you want to use real Google Maps data, you can optionally add a Google Places API key in Settings — but it's not required to get started.
Is my lead data private? ▼
Yes. All leads are stored locally in a JSON file on your computer. Nothing is ever sent to an external server. You own all your data completely.
Can I use this on mobile? ▼
Yes! LeadFlow is a Progressive Web App (PWA). When you run it on a phone's browser (connected to the same Wi-Fi), you can add it to your homescreen and use it like a native mobile app.
How do I sell this to different categories? ▼
Simply approach businesses in a specific niche (e.g. restaurants or salons in your city), run LeadFlow for their competitors' category, and offer the leads as a monthly service. Package it as "Lead Generation as a Service" — common pricing is ₹5,000–₹25,000/month per client depending on lead volume and niche.
What industries can I target? ▼
12 pre-built segments are included: Food & Restaurants, Healthcare, Real Estate, IT/Tech, Education, Retail, Automotive, Legal & Finance, Construction, Travel & Hospitality, Beauty, and Manufacturing. You can also add custom search terms for any niche.
Can I white-label this for my agency? ▼
Yes! The Agency and Enterprise licenses include custom branding rights. You can change the app name, logo, and color theme to match your agency's brand, and even resell it under your own product name with the Enterprise license.
